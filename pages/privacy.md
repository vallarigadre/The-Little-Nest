*Last updated: 25 November 2026*

This policy explains what information The Little Nest (thelittlenest.blog) collects when you visit, how it is used and the choices you have. The Little Nest is written by Vallari, a mom of a toddler. If you have a question about anything here, please use the [contact page](/contact/).

## Information you give us

- **Nest Notes sign-ups.** If you join the Sunday letter, we store your email address, the date you signed up and the page you signed up on. It is used only to send you the letter, and you can ask to be removed at any time.
- **Contact form.** If you send a message through the [contact page](/contact/), we store your name, email address and message so we can reply. We do not use it for anything else.

This information is stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare, and is never sold or shared with anyone for their own marketing.

## Site statistics

We count page views, how far readers scroll through a post, which product links are clicked and which site or app a visit came from (for example Pinterest or Threads). These counts are anonymous: we do not set cookies for them, and we do not store your IP address or anything that identifies you. We record only your general device type (phone or computer) and country.

## Cookies and third parties

**Amazon.** The Little Nest is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. When you click a link to Amazon.com, Amazon may set a cookie so it can credit this site if you make a purchase. That cookie is set and managed by Amazon under its own [privacy notice](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=468496). See our [affiliate disclosure](/disclosure/) for more.

**Beacons.** Our printables are sold and delivered through Beacons. When you visit our shop there, Beacons handles your order and payment details under its own privacy policy.

**Advertising.** The Little Nest may show ads served by Google and other advertising partners. These partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet:

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices) or, in Europe, [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu).
- To learn more, see [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them.

## Children's privacy

The Little Nest is written for parents and caregivers. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you (your newsletter email or a message you sent) at any time through the [contact page](/contact/). Depending on where you live, for example in California or the European Union, you may have further rights under local law, and we will honor them. We do not sell personal information.

## Keeping your information

Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to reply and keep a record of the conversation.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
