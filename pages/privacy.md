This page explains what information The Little Nest collects when you visit.

## Newsletter sign-ups

If you join the Sunday letter, your email address is stored securely by our hosting provider, Netlify, and used only to send you the newsletter. You can ask to be removed at any time by replying to any letter.

## Affiliate links and cookies

Product links to Amazon.com may place a cookie in your browser so Amazon can credit this site if you make a purchase. That cookie is set and managed by Amazon under its own privacy policy.

## Analytics

The site may use privacy-friendly analytics to count page views. These counts are aggregated and are not used to identify you.

## Contact

For privacy questions, reply to any Sunday letter and we will get back to you.
