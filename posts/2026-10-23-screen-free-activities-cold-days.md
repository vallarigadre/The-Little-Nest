There is a moment every cold-weather mom knows. It is 9:40 in the morning, it is drizzling, the toys have all been dumped out twice, and the TV remote is looking very tempting.

Screens are not the enemy, and some days they are exactly what everyone needs. But if you want a few more tricks in your pocket for the long indoor months ahead, this list is for you. Every idea is simple, most use things already in your kitchen, and many buy you a solid 20 minutes of focused play.

A quick safety note before we start: supervise anything with small parts, keep dried beans, rice and pasta away from children who still mouth objects, and always check age labels on toys.

## Sensory play

Sensory play is the secret weapon of indoor days. Toddlers can scoop, pour and dig for far longer than you would expect.

**1. Oat sensory bin.** Fill a shallow bin with dry rolled oats and add measuring cups and spoons. Oats are a great first filler because they are safe if a little ends up in a mouth.

**2. Water play in the kitchen.** Put a towel on the floor, add a baking dish of water, a few cups and a sponge. Pouring and squeezing will keep them going for ages.

**3. Ice excavation.** Freeze a few toy animals in a container of water overnight. Give your toddler warm water in a squeeze bottle to "rescue" them.

**4. Pom pom transfer.** Large pom poms, a muffin tin and a pair of chunky tongs. Sorting by color is a bonus.

## Busy hands

These are the quieter activities, perfect for when you need to cook dinner or answer an email.

**5. Sticker lines.** Draw a few straight lines on paper and ask your toddler to put dot stickers along them. Great for little fingers.

**6. Posting game.** Cut a slot in the lid of an oatmeal container and let them post jumbo craft sticks or large plastic coins through it.

**7. Play dough with tools.** Cookie cutters, a small rolling pin and a plastic knife turn play dough into a whole morning. The [Play-Doh starter set](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Play-Doh+toddler+starter+set) is a good place to begin.

**8. Busy board.** A [toddler busy board](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=montessori+busy+board+toddler) with buckles, zips and latches is one of the best investments for long winters, and it doubles as a car or travel toy.

**9. Tape rescue.** Tape a few small toys to a tray or table with painter's tape and let your toddler peel them free.

**10. Felt board stories.** Felt shapes stick to a felt board without glue. Toddlers love building faces, houses and animals. A [felt board story set](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=felt+board+story+set+toddler) is easy to bring out on any rainy day.

## Movement games

Cold days do not have to mean still bodies. A little movement before lunch can make nap time go much better.

**11. Cushion obstacle course.** Pillows to climb over, a blanket tunnel to crawl through and a laundry basket to step into.

**12. Animal walks.** Waddle like a penguin, hop like a bunny, stomp like an elephant. Take turns choosing the animal.

**13. Freeze dance.** Play music, dance, and freeze when it stops. Toddlers find this endlessly funny.

**14. Balloon keep-up.** Try to keep a balloon off the floor using hands, heads or a paper plate paddle. Supervise closely and put popped balloon pieces away right away.

**15. Indoor climbing.** If you have the space, a foldable [Pikler triangle](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=pikler+triangle+foldable+toddler) lets toddlers climb, slide and build confidence safely inside.

## Quiet time and calm-down ideas

Late afternoon is the hardest stretch of a cold day. These help everyone slow down.

**16. Book basket picnic.** Spread a blanket, add a basket of picture books and a snack, and read together on the floor.

**17. Flashlight hunt.** Close the curtains, hide a few toys and go looking for them with a flashlight.

**18. Bath time art.** Bath crayons or foam letters turn an early bath into play time.

**19. Window watching.** Count the cars, birds or raindrops. Name the colors you see. Simple, calm and surprisingly absorbing.

**20. Audio stories.** A screen-free [kids audio player](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=screen+free+audio+player+for+toddlers) with stories and songs gives you a breather without a screen.

## Make it easier on yourself

**Rotate, do not add.** Keep a few activities in a closet and bring out two or three at a time. Old toys feel new again after a week away.

**Prep one busy bag on Sunday.** Fill a zip pouch with stickers, a small notebook and crayons. Grab it on the hard days.

**Lower your standards for mess.** A towel underneath and a quick wipe afterward is part of the activity.

**Get outside anyway.** Even ten minutes in a warm coat and rain boots resets everyone. Puddle jumping counts as an activity.
