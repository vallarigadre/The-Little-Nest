If you have a busy 18 month old and keep seeing "tot school" on Pinterest, you might be wondering if it is too early. It is not too early, but it does look different at this age.

At 18 months, tot school is not lessons. It is a few minutes of play that you set up on purpose, in the same place, at about the same time each day. Your toddler is learning to focus, to use their hands with more control, and to feel the comfort of a small routine. That is plenty.

Here is how to start gently, what to expect, and what you can safely skip.

## What tot school looks like at 18 months

Think of it as a short, calm play date with one simple activity. Most toddlers this age will focus for somewhere between two and ten minutes, and that is completely normal.

A good session usually looks like this:

1. **Same spot, same time.** A low table or a corner of the rug, right after breakfast or after nap.
2. **One activity on a tray.** Just one. Too many choices make it harder to settle.
3. **Show it slowly once.** Use your hands, not many words.
4. **Step back and let them explore.** Even if they do it "wrong."
5. **Stop while it is still fun.** Put it back on the shelf together.

That is the whole thing. If it lasts three minutes, that still counts.

## What to skip at this age

It helps to know what you do not need to do, so you can let go of the pressure.

- **Skip worksheets and flashcards.** Toddlers learn through their hands and senses, not paper drills.
- **Skip the alphabet and numbers as goals.** You can name letters and count out loud during play, but recognition is not the aim yet.
- **Skip long sessions.** Ten minutes is a great day. Two is fine too.
- **Skip comparing.** Every toddler is different, and the rhythm matters more than the result.

## Easy activities by skill

Rotate a few of these over the week. Each one uses simple things, and many use what you already have at home.

### Posting and dropping

Toddlers this age love putting things into other things. An object permanence box, where a ball drops in and rolls back out, is a classic first Montessori activity. The [Montessori Mama object permanence box](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DJ22T459?tag=creativedi03c-20) is a simple wooden one. You can also cut a slot in an oatmeal container lid and post large wooden coins or jar lids through it.

### Stacking and nesting

Stacking cups build hand control and early ideas about size. Soft [Lalo silicone stacking cups](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DJC7WGMM?tag=creativedi03c-20) work for play and bath time. Show your toddler how to stack two, then let them knock the tower down. That is part of the fun.

### Shapes and simple sorting

Start with one shape at a time. A circle is easiest. A simple sorter like the [Melissa & Doug First Play Spin and Feed Shape Sorter](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0876GKD8F?tag=creativedi03c-20), made for ages 1 and up, is a gentle step up. A chunky knob puzzle like the [Melissa & Doug First Shapes Jumbo Knob Puzzle](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000EK59H0?tag=creativedi03c-20) is another favorite. Check the age label on any puzzle and pick the biggest knobs you can find.

### Scribbling

Toddlers this age hold crayons in their fist, so chunky shapes are easier. [Crayola My First Palm Grip Crayons](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0197UCD1M?tag=creativedi03c-20) are designed for that grip. Tape a big sheet of paper to the table so it does not slide, and let them scribble freely.

### Books and naming

Reading counts as tot school too. A sturdy book with real photos, like [First 100 Words by Roger Priddy](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0312510780?tag=creativedi03c-20), is great for pointing and naming. Ask "Where is the ball?" and celebrate when they point.

## A simple week for an 18 month old

If you like a little plan, here is one easy rhythm. Keep the same activity for a few days if your toddler enjoys it. Repetition is how they learn.

- **Monday:** Read a book together and name the pictures.
- **Tuesday:** Posting or dropping (object permanence box or a slot in a lid).
- **Wednesday:** Stacking cups or blocks.
- **Thursday:** Scribbling with chunky crayons.
- **Friday:** Music and movement: clap, march, dance with scarves.

## Setting up a tiny tot school corner

You only need a little space. A low shelf at your toddler's height, with two or three activities on it, invites them to choose. Wooden trays, like these [Montessori wooden trays with handles](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09X4896T3?tag=creativedi03c-20), show your toddler where each activity lives and make tidying up part of the routine.

![A low wooden shelf with three wooden trays holding a stacking toy, a shape sorter and a picture book in soft daylight](/images/tot-school-18-month-olds-3.jpg)

A quick safety note: at 18 months, everything still goes in the mouth. Keep anything small enough to fit through a toilet paper tube out of reach, check age labels, and stay close during every activity.
